# Galleypost — Studio demonstration guide

This is the run-sheet for the Studio (Dashboard, Studio, Assistant, Library, Analytics). The older Weekly Posts view is `DEMO-GUIDE.md` / **Classic view**.

Studio buttons that call the model are live OpenAI. Each click costs a little and results differ from run to run. If the key is missing, use Classic view.

## Before the meeting

1. Use the same browser and address you will present from.
2. Have `APP_PASSWORD` if you present a hosted copy.
3. Sidebar should show live OpenAI connected. If it says no key, add `OPENAI_API_KEY` and restart.
4. Rehearse once. A full run is a few cents of API use.
5. Prepare the Library with a few community photos (meetup room, laptops, venue). Tick **Cleared for public use** only when that is true.
6. Pre-make slow images if you can; keep one slot to generate live.
7. Backup: Classic view needs no key.

## Suggested sequence

1. **Dashboard.** An AI content desk: research, write, fact-check, brief the image; a person approves every post.
2. **Studio → Find latest topics.** One research search, about 15 seconds. Open source facts. Point at **Source confirmed**.
3. **Write my week.** Auto-fill, then write. Show the green fact-checked note and any amber “should check” note.
4. **Change the theme or tone.** Shorter, or “what it means for members”. Undo last revision.
5. **Images.** Show a finished image, then make one live.
6. **Library photo.** Overlay headline and logo; Position and Zoom. Generate around photo if you have time.
7. **Assistant.** Ask for a seasonal greeting or a thank-you. Attach a public PDF, not an internal record.
8. **Analytics.** Numbers are brought in by the team (link or screenshot). LinkedIn does not grant tools silent analytics access.
9. **Post to LinkedIn.** Opens the composer. Do not press Post unless you mean it.

## What to say clearly

- Posts may only say what their source facts say. A person still reviews every post.
- Images are AI illustrations unless you used a real library photo.
- The Studio never publishes on its own.
- Ideas, posts and Library photos stay in that browser on that address.
